# SEC-11: Access requests

**Category:** Policy | **Owner team:** Security | **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-07

**§11.1 Requests.** Requests for access, permissions or admin rights are IT requests. They are never declined as out of scope.

**§11.2 Approval.** Access to a shared drive or application needs approval from the data owner or the person's manager, recorded on the ticket.

**§11.3 Who changes groups.** Only a technician may change AD group membership.
